# Q50 — Amend Q37 and Q49: should every plan preview as a smaller version of what it makes?

## Ruling

*Ruling (2026-10-11):* **yes: a plan shows as a smaller, see-through
version of the thing it plans.** Made under a new number because the
rulings it amends are closed records: [Q37](question-answered-0037.md)
previewed a paint plan as the color at half alpha over the whole tile and
a building plan as a full-size ghost, and [Q49](question-answered-0049.md)
used a smaller square only over a tile already painted. Seen in play, the
smaller square was the version that read best, and it reads as "planned,
not yet made" on any tile.

### The rules

All are the renderer's and reach no hash.

1. **A paint plan** is a square of the new color inset from the tile's
   edge, nearly opaque, on a painted tile or an unpainted one alike; any
   paint already there shows round it.
2. **A building plan** is a translucent ghost of the building it plans —
   a printer for a printer plan, a crate for a depot plan — scaled down and
   standing on the tile.
3. **A plan to clear** a slot still hatches it at full size (Q37), and an
   overlay plan is still its marker and label.

## Worksheet

The argument, not the conclusion — options weighed, paths not taken.
Cite the ruling above; open this half only to recover *why*.

| Option | What it costs |
|---|---|
| **Smaller versions everywhere** *(chosen)* | A plan covers less of its tile, so a large painted area reads as a grid of squares until it is painted; bought: one look for every plan, distinct from the real thing at a glance. |
| Full-size half-alpha ghosts (Q37) | A plan over paint mixed the two colors, and a ghost read too much like the thing itself. |
| Smaller only over paint (Q49) | Two looks for one kind of plan. |

### How the answer took its shape

The smaller square came from Q49's fix for a paint plan over paint; it
was liked enough to become the rule. Making building plans match showed
that a printer plan had been drawn as a depot's crate, so it now draws as
a printer.
