# Q47 — Amend Q45: do printers and depots show their team by lights too?

## Ruling

*Ruling (2026-10-11):* **yes: a printer's and a depot's lights glow in
their team's color, as a bot's do; only a site keeps an outline.** Made
under a new number because the ruling it amends,
[Q45](question-answered-0045.md), is a closed record. Its rule 2 kept
buildings outlined only because the bots were done first.

### The rules

All are the renderer's and reach no hash.

1. **A printer's lights glow in its team's color**: the control-panel
   light on its front, the marker light on each side and the status
   light on its top lip. Its bands stay the neutral body, as Q43 rules
   for a building.
2. **A depot carries a team lamp** where the braces of its crate cross,
   glowing in its team's color. The depot's mesh shows its art whole
   only on top, so the lamp reads from above.
3. **A site keeps Q43's outline.** It is a translucent shape with no art
   to light; once built, the building it becomes shows its team by its
   lights.

## Worksheet

The argument, not the conclusion — options weighed, paths not taken.
Cite the ruling above; open this half only to recover *why*.

| Option | What it costs |
|---|---|
| **Lights on printers and depots, an outline on sites** *(chosen)* | Two ways to show a team, one of them for a passing state; bought: every finished machine reads the same way, and no outline crowds a base. |
| Outlines on every building (Q45 as ruled) | Bots and buildings show a team two different ways, and a base is ringed in outlines. |
| Lights on sites too | A site is a translucent ghost with no surface for a light, and it lasts only until built. |

### How the answer took its shape

The printer's art already had a control-panel light, side markers and
status lights in the swapped accent palette, so marking them as lights
was enough. The crate had none; a lamp where the braces cross reads
from above on the one face the depot's mesh shows whole. The bake's
light mask, written for the bot's atlas, now serves any art that marks
a light.
